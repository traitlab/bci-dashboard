About

What each page is for

  • Browse photos. Explore families, genera and species using Pl@ntNet first guesses or recorded labels, then inspect the photos.
  • Drone map. Find photos by drone photo location, then inspect their visual matches.
  • Similar photos. Compare a selected photo with Pl@ntNet’s stored visual matches. Looking alike does not confirm the same species.
  • Labelling queue. Choose the next batch for expert labelling and inspect why those photos come first.
  • Model health. Check how well Pl@ntNet names BCI trees, by species and overall, and what the evaluation covers.

Read the numbers with care

Photo counts do not measure tree abundance.

Model guesses need expert confirmation.

Each page describes its own snapshot, so the dates differ from page to page.

Glossary

Recorded label
The species name a botanist has recorded for a photo.
Pl@ntNet first guess
The species Pl@ntNet ranks first for a photo. It is a suggestion, not a confirmed name.
Labelled frame
A drone photo that carries a recorded label.
